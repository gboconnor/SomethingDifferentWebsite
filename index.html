When effort isn't the problem

Most organisations don't fail for lack of trying. They work hard, spend money, launch things, and still don't see the growth they expect. The issue is almost always direction and focus: too many priorities, no clear view of the real problem, and decisions made before the root cause is understood.

Something Different is built to fix exactly that.

What I do

From a stalled, noisy situation to a focused plan you can act on

1

Get to the root

Performance problems are rarely surface-level. I diagnose what's actually holding you back, looking across your customers, category, culture and company, separating symptoms from causes and bringing objectivity where politics or habit have blurred the picture.

2

Define the focus

Once the real constraint is clear, I help you make the few choices that matter most: where to compete, who for, and where to concentrate effort for the biggest commercial return. Focus beats activity every time.

3

Build the plan

A clear, practical plan your team can actually execute, with priorities, measures, and a simple way to track whether it's working. No jargon, no 90-slide decks nobody reads.

4

Steward the direction

Strategy fails when it's abandoned at the first sign of pressure. I stay close to make sure the direction holds and keeps delivering over time.
