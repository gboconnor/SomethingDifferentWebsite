# Something Different: static website

A clean, fast, self-contained website: homepage, contact page, and a searchable Musings archive of all 77 posts. No build tools, no monthly platform fee, host anywhere.

## Files

```
something-different-site/
├── index.html            Homepage (new copy)
├── contact.html          Contact page + enquiry form
├── musings.html          Searchable / filterable archive of all musings
├── musings/              The 77 individual article pages (generated)
├── musings_src/          Markdown source for each article (edit these)
├── build_site.py         Builds article pages + refreshes the archive
├── musings.json          The post data (generated, human-readable)
└── assets/
    ├── styles.css        All styling. Brand colours are at the very top.
    ├── logo.png          Your logo
    ├── gareth.jpg        Your portrait
    └── musings-data.js   The post data the archive page reads (generated)
```

## Preview it now

Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. Everything works offline except the Google fonts (which just fall back to system fonts without internet).

## Put it live (about 5 minutes, free)

The easiest route is **Netlify Drop**:

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the whole `something-different-site` folder onto the page.
3. It's live on a temporary URL instantly. In Netlify's site settings you can then point your `somethingdifferent.co.nz` domain at it.

(Any static host works too, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, or your existing host.)

## The contact form (Netlify Forms)

The form is already wired for Netlify Forms — no third-party account needed. Netlify
detects it automatically on deploy. One setup step to get the email notifications:

1. In Netlify, open your site → **Site configuration → Forms → Form notifications**.
2. Add an **email notification** and enter: gareth@somethingdifferent.co.nz
3. Submissions also appear any time under the site's **Forms** tab.

After someone submits, they land on `thanks.html` (a friendly confirmation page).

**Into Folk:** Netlify Forms feeds Folk via Zapier or Make — trigger: *New Netlify form
submission* → action: *Create/Update person in Folk*. Or simply add the person to Folk
from the notification email. Netlify Forms is the letterbox; Folk stays your CRM.

## The musings

All 77 musings now live on this site as real pages under `musings/`, built from
the markdown source files in `musings_src/`. The searchable archive (`musings.html`)
and each article page are generated by `build_site.py`.

### Adding or editing a musing

1. Create (or edit) a file `musings_src/your-url-slug.md` with this frontmatter:
   ```
   ---
   title: "Your title"
   category: "Marketing Strategy"
   date: "2026-08-05"
   dateDisplay: "5 Aug 2026"
   readMin: 6
   hero: "https://link-to-a-header-image.jpg"
   ---

   Your article text in markdown. Headings, **bold**, lists and images all work.
   ```
2. Run `python3 build_site.py` in this folder.
3. Re-upload the folder to your host.

That rebuilds the article page and refreshes the archive automatically — no HTML editing.

> Images: article images (heros and in-body) are loaded from your existing DatoCMS
> media library. They'll keep working as long as that library stays live. If you
> ever close the DatoCMS account, we'd first copy the images into `assets/` — easy
> to do, just ask.

## Brand colours

The site uses your official brand colours from the brand guide: Brand Pink `#E31B59`
and Brand Green `#004240`. They're set as `--pink` and `--green` in the first block of
`assets/styles.css`, change those two hex codes if you ever need to and the whole site updates.
