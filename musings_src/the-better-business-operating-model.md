**Better businesses aren't built department by department. They're built system by system.**

Organisations pursue different outcomes. Growth. Profitability. Resilience. Loyalty. Market leadership. But too often, they try to achieve them by improving individual functions in isolation.

The Better Business Operating Model offers a different way to think about organisational performance.

It brings together ideas from strategy, behavioural science, marketing effectiveness, customer experience, and organisational performance into a practical framework for understanding how stronger organisations are deliberately built.

At its heart are seven interconnected building blocks: **Purpose, Value, Memory, Preference, Access, Experience,** and **Consistency.** Together, they provide a way to diagnose where performance is being created, where it is being constrained, and where to focus next.

Because every organisation has an operating model.

**The question is whether it was designed deliberately or emerged by accident.**
