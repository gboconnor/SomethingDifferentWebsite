What do you believe?    In business, this matters.    What you believe shapes the values and principles that guide you and how you behave, not just externally with customers, suppliers, and partners, but internally with each other.    What you believe shapes the products, services, experiences you build and the value you offer.    What you believe and build then shapes how you brand and present yourself and the value you offer to the world.    What you believe matters.
