There is so much you could do when it comes to communications and embracing the Promotional 'P' of marketing.

It's safe to say much of what you could do; you probably shouldn't.

With that in mind, following my previous post on 'The Magnificent 7 Marketing Ideal-dos,' here are 'The 7 Communications Ideal-dos.'

If you're going to do something, it may as well be magnificent.

**The Magnificent 7 Communications Ideal-dos.**

**1. Build effective and efficient reach** - maximise your opportunity to maintain/grow market share.

**2. Use media placements that maximise attention** - create better cut-through and impact to increase attention.

**3. Get creative** - creativity helps to create disproportionate returns and makes every $ work harder.

**4. Ensure you are more distinctive than your** **competition** - make sure you stand out from the crowd and aren't ignored.

**5. Use emotion to your advantage** - emotion better connects when priming future buyers - current buyers like it, too.

**6. Get motivational without dropping your prices** - convert more current buyers with motivational comms showing you fulfil buyer's needs.

**7. Be great at being consistent** - stay the course and build greater brand salience over the long-term.
